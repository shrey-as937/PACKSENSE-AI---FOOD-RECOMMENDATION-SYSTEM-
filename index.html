⚠️ The Challenge

Why Food Spoils in Wrong Packaging

Small-scale farmers and SMEs lack access to packaging science, resulting in massive food waste due to incorrect material selection.

🥬

Fresh Produce

Fruits & vegetables suffocate in airtight packaging due to blocked respiration

Spoilage Risk: 85%
🍟

Fried & Fatty Foods

Chips, namkeen turn rancid from oxygen exposure and lose crunch from moisture

Rancidity Risk: 72%

High-Moisture Foods

Paneer, meat spoil from microbial growth without proper vacuum or MAP

Microbial Risk: 93%
⚠️

The Critical Gap

No accessible, affordable system exists to match food commodities with scientifically appropriate packaging specifications based on OTR, WVTR, film gauge, and barrier properties.
